Questions

Frequently asked.

How do I know if my house needs reblocking?+

Common signs are doors and windows that stick or won't close, sloping or bouncy floors, gaps between skirting boards and the floor, and cracks in plaster walls or cornices. If you can see rotten timber stumps under the house or the verandah is sinking, the stumps have failed and a reblock is due.

How long does reblocking take?+

Most standard houses take 1 to 2 weeks from first dig to final clean-up, depending on stump count, access, and the level of correction needed. We give you a clear schedule with your quote so you know exactly when we're in and out.

How much does reblocking cost?+

Price depends on the number of stumps, the size of the house, access, and how much levelling is required. Most jobs sit in a predictable range and we provide a fixed written quote after a free on-site visit. No hidden extras.

Do I need to move out during the work?+

Usually no. The house stays liveable for most jobs, with short windows where one section is jacked. We work around your family and let you know in advance when access to a room will be limited.

Do you handle the permits?+

Yes. As a Registered Building Practitioner we organise all permits, engineering and inspections required by your council. You don't chase paperwork - we do it.

What is your guarantee?+

Every reblock and restump is backed by our 15-year work guarantee. Concrete stumps don't rot, and our levelling holds. If something isn't right, we come back and sort it.

Do you service my area?+

We service Macedon Ranges and surrounding towns (Kyneton, Woodend, Gisborne, Romsey, Riddells Creek, Lancefield), Ballarat, Brunswick (and Brunswick East and West) and Prahran, with capacity across Victoria. Call 0466 961 177 if you're outside these zones.

How quickly can you start?+

We usually book a free site visit within a few days. Once you accept the quote, most reblocks start within 2 to 4 weeks depending on workload. Urgent jobs (real-estate, structural) can often be brought forward.
