What We Do

One trade. Done properly. Backed for 15 years.

We do the job tradies and architects refer out. If your house has dropped, leaning, cracking or sitting on tired timber stumps, we fix it. Concrete stumps only, every job permitted, every job levelled to engineering spec.

Reblocking & Restumping

Replace failing timber stumps with new concrete stumps across the whole footprint. The single most common job we do, and the one with the longest payoff.

About the Business

A family business, 35 years deep into one trade.

We have been reblocking and underpinning Melbourne homes since the early 1990s. Brothers, family crew, the same hands on the tools every week. We do not subcontract the work out and we do not chase whatever job is in fashion. One trade, done properly.

That is why a 15-year written guarantee on every job actually means something. We are still here. We still answer the phone. And the homes we reblocked in the 90s are still sitting straight.
