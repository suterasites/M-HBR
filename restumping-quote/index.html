Questions

Restumping FAQ.

How do I know if my house needs restumping?+

Common signs you need a restump are doors and windows that stick or won't close, sloping or bouncy floors, gaps between skirting boards and the floor, and cracks in plaster walls or cornices. If you can see rotten timber stumps under the house or the verandah is sinking, the stumps have failed and a restump is due.

How long does restumping take?+

Most standard houses take 1 to 2 weeks to restump, from first dig to final clean-up, depending on stump count, access, and the level of correction needed. We give you a clear restumping schedule with your quote so you know exactly when we're in and out.

How much does restumping cost in Melbourne?+

Restumping cost depends on the number of stumps, the size of the house, access, and how much levelling is required. Most Melbourne restumping jobs sit in a predictable range and we provide a fixed written quote after a free on-site visit. No hidden extras.

Do I need to move out during restumping?+

Usually no. The house stays liveable for most restumping jobs, with short windows where one section is jacked. We work around your family and let you know in advance when access to a room will be limited.

Do you handle the permits for restumping?+

Yes. As a Registered Building Practitioner we organise all restumping permits, engineering and inspections required by your council. You don't chase paperwork - we do it.

What is your restumping guarantee?+

Every restump is backed by our 15-year work guarantee. Concrete stumps don't rot, and our levelling holds. If something isn't right, we come back and sort it.

Where do you do restumping?+

We usually book a free site visit within a few days. Once you accept the restumping quote, most jobs start within 2 to 4 weeks depending on workload. Urgent restumps (real-estate, structural) can often be brought forward.
